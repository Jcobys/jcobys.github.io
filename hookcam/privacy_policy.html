We aim to collect and use only the information reasonably necessary to provide, maintain, secure, and improve the Services.

We may use information to provide requested features, respond to support requests, save or deliver content you ask us to process, maintain and improve the Services, diagnose technical problems, prevent abuse, comply with legal obligations, and protect the rights and safety of users and others.

We do not sell your personal information. You can control access to camera, microphone, photos, speech recognition, notifications, and other device features through your operating system settings. If you disable a permission, the related feature may not work, but the rest of the Service may remain available.

You may also manage browser cookies and analytics preferences through your browser settings. You can stop using a Service at any time and delete locally stored content from your device.

Content stored locally by an application remains on your device until you delete it or the operating system removes it. Information submitted in support requests is retained only as long as reasonably necessary to respond, maintain records, resolve disputes, or meet legal requirements.

To ask about personal information associated with a support request or to request deletion where applicable, contact us using the email address below.
