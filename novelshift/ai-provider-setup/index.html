NovelShift AI translation

Cloud translation is built into NovelShift. No personal API key, model configuration or separate AI provider account is needed.

Start translating
Import an EPUB or TXT book, choose translation or bilingual mode, and confirm cloud processing.
Free plan
50,000 source characters per calendar month, using Cloudflare Workers AI.
Monthly and yearly Pro
2,000,000 source characters per calendar month, using OpenAI through OpenRouter while your subscription is valid.
After expiry
New requests use the free route and its remaining allowance. Saved translations remain readable.

Check your network and remaining allowance if translation is unavailable. Manage or restore your subscription in the app. Cached translations do not consume the allowance again.

With your consent, the required text, titles and context are sent through Cloudflare to the AI services described above. Enabled automatic translation may process following chapters.
