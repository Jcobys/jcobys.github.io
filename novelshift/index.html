01Privacy Policy

Your reading is personal.

This Privacy Policy explains how NovelShift handles information when you use the app and this documentation site.

Data collection

NovelShift is designed for local reading. Books, reading progress, highlights, settings, and downloaded translations are stored on your device unless a feature clearly tells you otherwise. If you choose AI translation, the selected text and instructions needed to process it may be sent to the provider you choose. We do not sell personal information or use private reading content for advertising.

We may receive limited technical information needed to operate purchases, respond to support requests, prevent abuse, or comply with law. Optional diagnostics or analytics must be disclosed in the app and App Store privacy details before collection begins.

Cookies and local storage

The NovelShift app does not rely on web cookies to read your books. This website may use an essential preference cookie or local storage to remember document-navigation choices. It is not used for advertising or cross-site tracking. If non-essential cookies are added, we will ask for consent where required and update this policy first.

Data security and retention

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards appropriate to the information involved. No system is completely secure, so keep your device protected and do not share account or provider credentials. Data remains only as long as needed for the stated purpose, required by law, or retained locally by you. Deleting the app or a book removes device-local information subject to device backup settings.

Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, object to, or receive a copy of personal information we control. You may withdraw consent for optional processing at any time. To make a request, contact zlhibari@gmail.com. We may need to verify your request and will respond as required by law.

03Subscription Terms

Straightforward subscriptions.

These Subscription Terms apply to paid NovelShift features purchased through the Apple App Store. They supplement the Apple Media Services Terms and do not limit mandatory consumer rights.

What you will see before purchase

The in-app purchase screen will display the subscription name, duration, price, and any trial terms before you confirm. The exact plan shown in the App Store at purchase controls if it differs from a general description here.

Plans and billing

Paid plans unlock premium features and limits shown in the App Store purchase screen. Prices, currencies, taxes, trial eligibility, and available plans vary by country and are presented by Apple before purchase. Apple, not NovelShift, processes payment and provides your purchase receipt.

Automatic renewal

Unless you cancel, an auto-renewable subscription renews at the end of each billing period and your Apple Account is charged. You can manage or cancel in Apple Account Settings → Subscriptions. Cancellation takes effect after the current paid period ends; deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.

Refund policy

App Store purchases are billed by Apple. Refund eligibility and requests are handled by Apple under its policies and local law. You can request a refund through Apple’s Report a Problem flow or, where available, from the app. Contact NovelShift support for product help, but we cannot directly issue App Store refunds.

Service changes and termination

We may change, suspend, or discontinue features when reasonably necessary for security, legal compliance, maintenance, or product development. We will provide notice where required. We may suspend access for material misuse, fraud, or violation of these terms. If a paid service ends before a paid period, we will provide any remedy required by law or App Store rules.
