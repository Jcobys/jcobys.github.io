隐私政策

更新及生效日期：2026 年 11 月 9 日

本政策说明 RGB拾音灯如何处理麦克风音频、场景设置和购买信息。服务提供者为 App Store 页面及购买记录所列的开发者或销售方。

麦克风与音频

首次点按「开始拾音」时，应用会请求麦克风权限。应用在设备上实时分析环境声音，用于驱动灯效，不保存原始录音，也不上传音频。

点按「关闭拾音」或进入后台时，麦克风采集会停止。如果进入后台前正在拾音，返回应用后会尝试恢复。你可以在系统设置中关闭麦克风权限。

本地场景与设置

收藏的场景保存在设备上，包含场景名称、灯效、配色和调节参数。你可以在场景库中删除已收藏的场景。应用没有账号系统、广告或第三方统计。

专业版购买

专业版购买由 Apple 处理。应用使用 StoreKit 验证交易和购买权益，并支持恢复购买。应用不会读取银行卡等付款资料。Apple 隐私政策。

网站与联系

本政策页面由 GitHub Pages 托管。访问页面时，GitHub 可能处理提供网站服务所需的连接信息。本页面未加入广告或访客统计工具。GitHub 隐私声明。

如果你主动发邮件联系我们，我们会使用你提供的邮箱和邮件内容来答复和处理请求。隐私问题请联系 zlhibari@gmail.com。政策更新时会在本页标注日期。

Privacy Policy

Updated and effective: November 9, 2026

This policy explains how RGB拾音灯 handles microphone audio, scene settings, and purchase information. The service provider is the developer or seller identified on the App Store listing and purchase record.

Microphone and audio

The app requests microphone access the first time you start listening. It analyzes ambient sound on your device in real time to drive the lighting effects. It does not save raw recordings or upload audio.

Microphone capture stops when you turn off listening or the app enters the background. If capture was active before the app entered the background, the app attempts to resume it when you return. You can revoke microphone access in system settings.

Local scenes and settings

Saved scenes are stored on your device and contain the scene name, lighting effect, color palette, and adjustment settings. You can delete saved scenes from the scene library. The app has no account system, ads, or third-party analytics.

Pro purchases

Apple processes Pro purchases. The app uses StoreKit to verify transactions and purchase entitlements and to restore purchases. The app does not access card numbers or similar payment details. Apple Privacy Policy.

Website and contact

GitHub Pages hosts this policy. GitHub may process connection information needed to provide the website. This page does not include ads or visitor analytics. GitHub Privacy Statement.

If you email us, we use the email address and information you provide to respond and handle your request. For privacy questions, contact zlhibari@gmail.com. We date policy updates on this page.
