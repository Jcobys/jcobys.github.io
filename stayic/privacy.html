隐私政策

更新及生效日期：2026 年 10 月 1 日

StayIC 重视你的隐私。本政策说明你使用 StayIC App、键盘及本网站时，哪些信息会被处理、处理目的，以及你可以如何管理这些信息。服务提供者为 App Store 页面及购买记录所列的开发者／销售方。如有疑问，请联系 zlhibari@gmail.com。

1. 我们处理的信息

  • 你保存的内容：角色名称、头像、描述、规则、来源资料，以及相关设置和使用记录，用于建立和管理你的角色库。
  • 你提交给 AI 的内容：提取规则时提交的角色描述；检查时提交的文字及所选角色的名称和相关规则；生成角色资料时输入的名称、作品或版本信息。
  • 购买信息：购买状态及恢复购买所需的信息，用于提供 Pro 权益。支付由 Apple 处理，我们不接收你的完整支付卡号。
  • 服务运行信息：使用在线功能或访问本网站时产生的 IP 地址、请求时间、服务状态及用量信息，用于提供服务、处理故障和防止滥用。
  • 支持信息：你联系我们时主动提供的邮箱、问题描述及附件，用于答复和处理请求。

2. 信息的使用目的

我们仅为提供角色管理、规则提取、对话检查、资料生成、同步和购买服务，维护服务安全，以及处理支持请求和履行法律义务而使用相关信息。我们不会出售你的个人信息，也不将角色资料或输入文字用于广告定向。

角色库主要保存在你的设备上。使用本地编辑或键盘输入，并不会自动将这些内容发送给 AI 服务。

3. AI 功能与第三方服务

AI 功能为可选功能。你主动使用时，完成该功能所需的提交内容会由在线服务及相关服务商处理。角色资料生成还会使用你输入的查询信息检索公开资料。请只提交你有权使用的信息，避免包含密码、支付资料或不必要的敏感个人信息。

  • AI 与资料检索：OpenRouter、OpenAI 和 Exa 提供相关 AI 或搜索服务，处理该功能所需的文字或查询信息。
  • 在线服务运行：Cloudflare 提供在线服务支持，可能处理请求内容及必要的连接和服务运行信息。
  • 购买与可选同步：Apple 处理支付；你开启 iCloud 同步时，相关角色资料会保存到你的 iCloud，并通过你的 Apple 账户同步。
  • 网站访问：GitHub 提供网站托管服务，可能处理访客的连接信息。本网站不使用广告或访客追踪工具。

这些服务商可能在你所在国家或地区之外处理信息，其保留和使用规则见下方隐私政策。我们不会将你提交的文字用于训练我们自己的 AI 模型；第三方服务的数据保留及模型训练规则，请以其政策为准。取消操作或关闭 App 无法收回已经提交给在线服务的信息。

服务商隐私政策：Apple · Cloudflare · OpenRouter · OpenRouter 数据使用说明 · OpenAI · Exa · GitHub。

4. 键盘与系统权限

StayIC 键盘用于文字输入、查看角色规则及主动发起对话检查。检查时会读取当前输入框可提供的文字；我们不会在后台收集你的输入历史或监控所有对话。部分 App 和安全输入框可能限制第三方键盘的使用。

键盘中的在线 AI 功能需要在 iOS 设置中开启“完全访问”。开启该权限不会自动上传你输入的全部文字。你可以随时关闭完全访问或移除 StayIC 键盘；这可能使相关键盘功能无法使用。

添加头像或导入资料时，我们仅处理你主动选择或提供的内容。

5. 同步、导出与分享

iCloud 同步由你选择开启。关闭同步会停止后续同步，但不会自动删除此前保存在 iCloud 或备份中的内容。

你主动导出或分享角色时，文件可能包含头像、描述、规则和来源资料，并交给你选择的接收方。请在分享前检查内容。你在其他 App、文件或服务中保存的副本，需在相应位置另行管理。

6. 保存期限与信息安全

你保存在设备上的角色资料会保留到你删除为止，备份和同步副本按你的相关设置保留。我们不会建立包含你的全部键盘输入历史的档案。支持通信及必要的服务运行记录仅在处理请求、维护安全或履行法律义务所需的期间保留。第三方处理的信息按其政策和适用规则保留。

我们采取合理的保护措施防止未经授权的访问、泄露或滥用，但任何存储或传输方式都无法保证绝对安全。请妥善保护设备和 Apple 账户。

7. 你的选择与权利

你可以编辑或删除角色、导出资料、停止使用 AI、关闭 iCloud 同步，并在 iOS 设置中管理键盘权限。删除 App 不会自动取消订阅，也不一定删除 iCloud、备份、已分享文件或第三方保留的信息；请在相应服务中管理这些内容。

依适用法律，你可以请求访问、更正或删除我们持有的个人信息，提出限制处理、撤回同意或其他隐私请求。请通过 zlhibari@gmail.com 联系我们。我们可能需要必要的信息核实请求，但不会要求你提供密码或完整支付资料。撤回同意不影响撤回前依法进行的处理。

8. 未成年人

未成年人应在监护人的指导和依法需要的同意下使用 StayIC。我们不会有意收集儿童的个人信息。监护人认为儿童向我们提供了个人信息时，可联系我们协助处理。

9. 政策更新与联系

我们可能随功能或法律要求变化更新本政策，并在本页标明日期。涉及重大变化时，我们会依法提供通知，并在需要时重新征求同意。

隐私问题和请求请联系：zlhibari@gmail.com。

Privacy Policy

Updated and effective: October 1, 2026

StayIC respects your privacy. This policy explains the information processed when you use the StayIC app, keyboard and website, why it is processed, and your choices. The service provider is the developer/seller identified on the App Store listing and purchase record. Contact us at zlhibari@gmail.com.

1. Information we process

  • Content you save: Character names, avatars, descriptions, rules, source references, settings and usage records, to create and manage your library.
  • Content you submit to AI: Descriptions for rule extraction; text, the selected character’s name and relevant rules for checks; and names, works or versions for character profile generation.
  • Purchase information: Purchase status and information needed to restore purchases and provide Pro access. Apple processes payments. We do not receive your full payment-card number.
  • Service information: IP addresses, request times, service status and usage information arising from online features or website visits, to deliver the service, resolve problems and prevent abuse.
  • Support information: Your email address, message and attachments that you choose to send when contacting us.

2. How we use information

We use relevant information to provide character management, rule extraction, dialogue checks, profile generation, sync and purchase services; maintain security; respond to support requests; and meet legal obligations. We do not sell your personal information or use character content or typed text for targeted advertising.

Your character library is primarily stored on your device. Local editing and keyboard typing do not automatically send that content to AI services.

3. AI features and service providers

AI features are optional. When you choose to use them, the content needed for the feature is processed by online services and the relevant providers. Profile generation also uses your query to search public sources. Submit only information you have permission to use, and avoid passwords, payment details or unnecessary sensitive personal information.

  • AI and research: OpenRouter, OpenAI and Exa provide AI or search services and process the text or queries needed for the feature.
  • Online service operation: Cloudflare supports online services and may process request content and necessary connection and service information.
  • Purchases and optional sync: Apple processes payments. If you enable iCloud sync, relevant character data is stored in your iCloud and synced through your Apple account.
  • Website visits: GitHub hosts this website and may process visitor connection information. This website does not use advertising or visitor tracking tools.

These providers may process information outside your country or region. Their retention and use practices are described in the policies below. We do not use your submitted text to train our own AI models; third-party retention and model-training practices are governed by their policies. Cancelling an action or closing the app cannot recall information already submitted to an online service.

Provider policies: Apple · Cloudflare · OpenRouter · OpenRouter data practices · OpenAI · Exa · GitHub.

4. Keyboard and system permissions

The StayIC keyboard supports typing, viewing character rules and checks that you initiate. A check reads the text available in the active input field. We do not collect your typing history in the background or monitor all conversations. Some apps and secure fields may restrict third-party keyboards.

Online AI features in the keyboard require Full Access in iOS Settings. Enabling this permission does not automatically upload everything you type. You can turn it off or remove the StayIC keyboard at any time; related keyboard features may then be unavailable.

When adding an avatar or importing material, we process only the content you choose or provide.

5. Sync, exports and sharing

You choose whether to enable iCloud sync. Turning it off stops future synchronization but does not automatically delete existing iCloud data or backups.

When you export or share a character, the file may include the avatar, description, rules and references and is sent to the destination you choose. Review its contents before sharing. Copies saved in other apps, files or services must be managed there separately.

6. Retention and security

Character content stored on your device remains until you delete it; backups and sync copies follow your settings. We do not maintain a record of your entire keyboard typing history. Support correspondence and necessary service records are kept for the time needed to handle requests, maintain security or meet legal obligations. Third-party information is retained according to the relevant provider’s policies and applicable rules.

We take reasonable measures to prevent unauthorized access, disclosure and misuse, but no storage or transmission method is completely secure. Protect your device and Apple account.

7. Your choices and rights

You can edit or delete characters, export data, stop using AI, turn off iCloud sync and manage keyboard permissions in iOS Settings. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription or necessarily remove iCloud data, backups, shared files or provider-held information. Manage these through the relevant services.

Subject to applicable law, you may request access, correction or deletion of personal information we hold, restrict processing, withdraw consent or make other privacy requests. Contact zlhibari@gmail.com. We may need limited information to verify your request, but will not ask for passwords or full payment details. Withdrawal does not affect lawful processing that took place beforehand.

8. Children

Minors should use StayIC with a parent or guardian’s guidance and any consent required by law. We do not knowingly collect children’s personal information. A parent or guardian who believes a child has provided personal information to us can contact us for assistance.

9. Updates and contact

We may update this policy as features or legal requirements change and will date updates on this page. For material changes, we will provide notice and seek renewed consent where required.

For privacy questions and requests, contact zlhibari@gmail.com.
