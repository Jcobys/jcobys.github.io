Provider and scope

StayIC is provided by the developer/seller identified on its App Store listing and purchase record. This policy covers the app, its keyboard extension, cloud AI features and these support pages. Contact: zlhibari@gmail.com. No separate StayIC account is required.

Your library and optional iCloud

Names, optional character gender, descriptions, avatars, rules, source references, settings and usage counts are stored on your device. The app and keyboard share the data needed for their features through an App Group. Character gender describes a character, rather than a request for your personal gender. If you enable iCloud sync, the applicable library data is stored in your private Apple iCloud database and synced using your Apple account. Device and iCloud backups may also contain app data according to your Apple settings.

Keyboard and Full Access

Typing, formatting and reading saved character rules do not require sending text to our AI service. The keyboard reads the available draft from the active input field when you request a check; it does not monitor all conversations or collect a background typing history. iOS and host apps can restrict available text, disable third-party keyboards or require switching to the system keyboard for secure fields. Full Access permits network requests and shared-container access; enabling it alone is not permission to automatically upload everything you type.

Data sent for AI requests

When you choose a cloud AI action, the request is sent over HTTPS to our Cloudflare Workers service, then to OpenRouter and the model provider used for that request. Rule extraction sends the description you submit. Character Check sends the character name, applicable rules and conditions, and the draft you ask to check; it does not send the entire character library or avatar. Profile generation sends the name and optional work/version you enter; this lookup uses Exa web search and public-source excerpts to generate a description and rules. Requests also contain a random request identifier; network infrastructure receives ordinary connection metadata, including IP address, for delivery and rate protection. Current cloud processing uses an OpenAI model through OpenRouter. Cloud AI is optional: avoid submitting passwords, financial details, sensitive private conversations or information about others without appropriate permission.

Processing, logs and retention

Our AI service does not maintain a stored conversation or character-library database. Its custom application logs exclude drafts, descriptions, prompts, access tokens and purchase payloads; they record request identifiers, operation/status, timing and numerical token/cost metrics for troubleshooting and cost control. Infrastructure security and operational records follow the hosting provider’s retention settings. Model and search providers have their own processing, security, retention and training practices; we do not promise that every upstream provider retains nothing or that all processing happens on your device. Review the linked provider policies below. Requests already transmitted cannot be recalled by closing the app or cancelling the local task.

Purchases and exports

Apple handles payment details. StayIC uses StoreKit-verified purchase information to provide and restore Pro access and stores the necessary entitlement state locally for the app and keyboard. We do not receive your full payment-card number. Exporting or sharing a character uses the destination you select in the system share sheet; the exported file may contain the avatar, description, rules and references. These pages do not automatically publish your characters. Copies saved or shared outside StayIC are controlled by you and the recipient’s services.

Your choices and privacy requests

You can use local features without cloud AI, stop making AI requests, turn off keyboard Full Access in iOS Settings, and turn off iCloud sync in the app. Disabling sync stops future synchronization; it does not by itself erase previously synced records or Apple backups. Delete characters in the app to remove active library records; manage remaining iCloud data and backups through Apple settings. Uninstalling alone is not a promise to erase iCloud data, exports or provider-held records. Contact support to request access, correction, deletion or information about processing, subject to applicable law and the data actually available to us. We may need limited information to verify a request; do not email passwords or private drafts. Support correspondence is retained as needed to handle the request and applicable legal obligations.

Hosting, transfers and children

These static pages are hosted on GitHub Pages and do not add analytics scripts, advertising, tracking pixels or external fonts. GitHub may process visitor connection data under its policy. Apple, Cloudflare, OpenRouter and downstream providers may process data in other countries; protections and rights depend on applicable law and provider arrangements. StayIC does not intentionally seek personal information from children. If a child has supplied personal information to us, a parent or guardian may contact support for help with removal.

Updates and provider policies

Material changes will be reflected in this policy and, where required, in an in-app notice or renewed permission before the changed processing starts. Provider links: Apple, Cloudflare, OpenRouter, OpenRouter model-provider data practices, OpenAI, Exa, GitHub.

服务提供者与范围

StayIC 由 App Store 页面及购买记录所列的开发者／销售方提供。本政策涵盖 App、键盘扩展、云端 AI 功能及本支持站点。联系邮箱：zlhibari@gmail.com。无需注册独立 StayIC 账号。

角色库与可选 iCloud

角色姓名、可选性别、描述、头像、规则、来源引用、设置及使用次数保存在设备上。App 与键盘通过 App Group 共享功能所需数据。性别描述的是角色，不要求提供你本人的性别。开启 iCloud 同步时，相关角色库数据存入你在 Apple iCloud 的私有数据库，通过你的 Apple 账户同步。设备和 iCloud 备份也可能根据 Apple 设置包含 App 数据。

键盘与完全访问

输入、格式调整及阅读已保存的角色规则无需向我们的 AI 服务发送文字。你主动请求检查时，键盘读取当前输入框可提供的草稿；不会监控所有会话或在后台收集输入历史。iOS 和宿主 App 可能限制可读文本、禁用第三方键盘，或在安全输入框切换到系统键盘。“完全访问”允许网络请求及共享容器访问；单独开启该权限不代表授权自动上传全部输入。

AI 请求发送的数据

你选择云端 AI 操作时，请求通过 HTTPS 发往我们的 Cloudflare Workers 服务，再由 OpenRouter 交给该请求使用的模型供应商。规则提取发送你提交的描述。角色检查发送角色名称、适用规则与条件，以及你要求检查的草稿；不发送整个角色库或头像。资料生成发送你输入的名称及可选作品／版本，通过 Exa 网络搜索及公开资料片段生成描述和规则。请求还包含随机请求标识；网络基础设施为传输和限流接收包括 IP 地址在内的常规连接信息。当前云端处理通过 OpenRouter 使用 OpenAI 模型。云端 AI 为可选功能：请勿发送密码、金融资料、敏感私人对话，或未经适当许可提供他人信息。

处理、日志与保留

我们的 AI 服务不建立已存储的会话或角色库数据库。自定义应用日志不包含草稿、描述、提示词、访问凭据或购买载荷，仅记录请求标识、操作／状态、耗时及数字形式的 token／费用指标，用于排障和成本管理。基础设施安全及运行记录按托管服务商的保留设置处理。模型与搜索服务商具有各自的处理、安全、保留及训练规则；我们不承诺所有上游零保留，也不承诺全部处理均在本机完成。请查阅下方服务商政策。已传出的请求不会因关闭 App 或取消本地任务而被收回。

购买与导出

Apple 处理支付资料。StayIC 使用经 StoreKit 验证的购买信息提供及恢复 Pro 权益，并在本地保存 App 与键盘所需的权益状态。我们不会接收你的完整支付卡号。导出或分享角色时，文件发往你在系统分享面板选择的目的地，可能包含头像、描述、规则及引用。本支持站点不会自动公开发布你的角色。在 StayIC 之外保存或分享的副本由你和接收方服务管理。

你的选择与隐私请求

你可以只使用本地功能、停止发起 AI 请求、在 iOS 设置中关闭键盘完全访问，并在 App 内关闭 iCloud 同步。关闭同步停止后续同步，不会自动清除此前已同步记录或 Apple 备份。在 App 内删除角色可移除活动角色库记录；其余 iCloud 数据与备份通过 Apple 设置管理。仅卸载 App 不代表会清除 iCloud 数据、导出文件或供应商持有记录。你可以联系支持，依适用法律和我们实际可访问的数据提出访问、更正、删除或处理说明请求。核实请求可能需要少量信息；请勿通过邮件发送密码或私人草稿。支持邮件按处理请求及适用法律义务所需保留。

网站、跨境处理与儿童

本静态站点由 GitHub Pages 托管，不添加统计脚本、广告、追踪像素或外部字体。GitHub 可能依其政策处理访客连接数据。Apple、Cloudflare、OpenRouter 及下游服务商可能在其他国家处理数据；保护措施及权利取决于适用法律和服务商安排。StayIC 不主动寻求收集儿童个人资料。如儿童向我们提供了个人资料，家长或监护人可联系支持协助删除。

更新及服务商政策

政策实质变更会在本页更新；依法需要时，在改变处理方式前通过 App 通知或重新征求许可。服务商政策：Apple、Cloudflare、OpenRouter、OpenRouter 模型供应商数据规则、OpenAI、Exa、GitHub。
