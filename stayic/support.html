购买与订阅

使用购买时的 Apple 账户，在 StayIC 中选择“恢复购买”以恢复符合条件的已购权益。

管理 Apple 订阅 · 申请 Apple 退款

使用键盘

在 iOS 设置 → 通用 → 键盘 → 键盘中添加 StayIC。键盘中的在线 AI 检查需要开启“完全访问”。部分 App 和安全输入框可能使用系统键盘。

你可以在同一设置中关闭完全访问或移除 StayIC 键盘。有关数据使用，请阅读隐私政策。

Support

For questions, purchase assistance, privacy requests or complaints, contact zlhibari@gmail.com. Include the StayIC version, iOS version, device model and a brief description. Send only what is needed to address the issue, without passwords, payment-card details or private conversations.

Purchases and subscriptions

Use Restore Purchases in StayIC with the Apple account used to buy your eligible purchases.

Manage Apple subscriptions · Request an Apple refund

Using the keyboard

Add StayIC in iOS Settings → General → Keyboard → Keyboards. Online AI checks in the keyboard require Full Access. Some apps and secure fields may use the system keyboard.

You can turn off Full Access or remove the StayIC keyboard in the same settings. See our Privacy Policy for information handling.
