隐私政策

更新及生效日期：2026 年 11 月 20 日

本政策说明 Wheek App 与本网站的信息处理方式。服务提供者为 App Store 页面及购买记录列明的开发者／销售方。

1. 本机保存的数据

你录入的小猪名字、性别、生日、到家日期、头像、照片、Live Photo、视频、日记、饮食、便便、体重、健康记录和提醒保存在本机，用于展示日历、相册、动态、照护提醒与日记导出。Wheek 无需注册独立账户。

2. 照片处理与权限

抠图贴纸由设备上的系统图像识别功能处理。相机仅在你主动拍照时使用；相册选择器只向 App 提供你选择的媒体。提醒需要通知权限，通知内容可能在锁屏显示。你可在系统设置调整相机、相册及通知权限，拒绝权限可能影响对应功能。

3. 备份、导出与保留

当前版本不提供 App 内自动 iCloud 同步。你可通过 Pro 手动导出备份、从备份恢复或导出 PDF。选择 iCloud Drive、邮件或其他分享目的地后，文件由你选择的服务及接收方处理，可能包含照片、记录和媒体原有元数据。设备备份也可能由 Apple 按你的系统设置处理。本机资料会保留到你删除或替换它们；卸载 App 可能移除本机资料。已分享或保存到其他位置的副本需另行删除，恢复备份会替换当前 App 数据。

4. 购买及第三方服务

Apple 通过 App Store 与 StoreKit 处理付款、交易验证、订阅及恢复购买。Wheek 处理相应购买状态以提供 Pro 权益，不接收完整支付卡号。Apple 的处理适用其隐私政策。联系支持时，你主动提供的邮箱、问题描述和附件用于答复及处理请求。网站由 GitHub Pages 托管，GitHub 可能处理 IP 地址等连接信息；语言选择会保存在浏览器本地。

5. 数据使用与分享

Wheek 不将你的日记和媒体自动上传到开发者服务器，不出售这些内容，不提供广告定向，也未集成第三方广告或行为追踪 SDK。使用系统分享、邮件支持或 Apple 服务时，会发生你主动选择的必要处理。相关服务可能在你所在地区之外处理数据。

6. 你的选择与权利

你可在 App 内查看、修改和删除记录，管理提醒，或调整系统权限。备份与 PDF 导出为 Pro 功能。关于支持通信中个人信息的访问、更正、删除或其他适用隐私权请求，可通过下方邮箱联系我们；我们会依法处理。保护你的设备与导出文件，避免分享不必要的个人信息。

7. 未成年人、政策更新与联系

未成年人应在监护人指导及依法所需同意下使用和购买。我们不主动要求儿童提交个人信息。政策更新会在本页标明日期，重大变化将依法提供通知。如有疑问，请联系下方支持邮箱。

Privacy Policy

Updated and effective: November 20, 2026

This policy describes information handling in the Wheek app and this website. The provider is the developer/seller identified on the App Store listing and purchase record.

1. Data stored on your device

Pet names, sex, birthdays, arrival dates, avatars, photos, Live Photos, videos, journal entries, feeding, droppings, weight and health records, and reminders are stored locally to provide the calendar, album, timeline, care reminders and journal exports. No separate Wheek account is required.

2. Media processing and permissions

Sticker extraction uses system image recognition on your device. The camera is used when you choose to take a photo; the system photo picker provides the media you select. Reminders require notification permission, and their contents may appear on your lock screen. Manage camera, photo and notification permissions in system settings; denying a permission can limit the corresponding feature.

3. Backups, exports and retention

The current version does not offer automatic in-app iCloud sync. Pro lets you manually export backups, restore a backup and export PDFs. If you choose iCloud Drive, email or another destination, that service and your recipients handle the files, which may include photos, records and original media metadata. Apple may also back up device data according to your system settings. Local data remains until you delete or replace it; uninstalling may remove it. Delete copies saved or shared elsewhere separately. Restoring a backup replaces the current app data.

4. Purchases and third-party services

Apple processes payments, transaction verification, subscriptions and restored purchases through the App Store and StoreKit. Wheek uses purchase status to provide Pro and does not receive full payment card details. Apple handles data under its privacy policy. When you contact support, the email address, description and attachments you provide are used to respond and handle your request. GitHub Pages hosts this website and may process connection information such as IP addresses. The website stores language preferences locally in your browser.

5. Data use and sharing

Wheek does not automatically upload your journal or media to developer servers, sell that content or use it for targeted advertising. It has no third-party advertising or behavioral tracking SDKs. System sharing, email support and Apple services involve the processing needed for the actions you choose. Providers may process information outside your region.

6. Your choices and rights

You can view, edit and delete records in the app, manage reminders and adjust system permissions. Backup and PDF export are Pro features. Contact us below to request access, correction, deletion or other applicable privacy rights concerning information in support communications. We will handle requests under applicable law. Protect your device and exported files, and avoid sharing unnecessary personal information.

7. Minors, changes and contact

Minors should use the app and make purchases with guardian guidance and any consent required by law. We do not intentionally ask children to submit personal information. Policy updates will carry a revised date, and material changes will be notified as required by law. Contact support below with questions.
